RUENTR

Content factory

One process from an idea to a caption under a post

  1. Market

    We study viral posts and videos in the niche, take the structure and the technique, and do not copy the text.

  2. Ideas and hooks

    A ready set of 50 hooks and post rules: headline, length, where to put the call to action.

  3. Text

    An AI copywriter writes, a critic scores it and sends weak texts back for rewriting.

  4. Carousels

    A generator builds the images: background plus slide text, in one style.

  5. Videos

    Seven video formats, one font, a cover and captions for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram*.

  6. Calendar

    A 90-day plan with rubrics so that content goes out regularly.

  7. Publishing

    Finished materials go to autoposting on schedule.

The working process by which we run our own channels.
