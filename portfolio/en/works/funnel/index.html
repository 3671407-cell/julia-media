How a person returns to the flow after the gift

  • Right after the reply: «Noted: {place}. I will read it personally» and video 2 arrives by itself. The person does not look for what is next.
  • If the map was not opened: after 10 minutes one soft reminder and the same video 2.
  • If they played but did not reply: video 2 still arrives 10 minutes after the map is given.
  • If they left completely: a day later «We stopped at the video. Shall we continue?» [Continue].
  • The gift is not lost: the button is in the bot menu and under video 2.

What changes for you

  • You get answers about places only from those who played: these are warm people to talk to.
  • Those who pressed «I want to travel» go into the funnel without the gift and do not have to wait.
  • There is no stream of «Message Julia» buttons in these blocks; it appears later, from block 4.
