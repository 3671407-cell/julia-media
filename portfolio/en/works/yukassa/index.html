Payment acceptance via YooKassa

The buyer's path from the button to access to the product

  1. Button

    A «Buy» button in the bot or on the product page.

  2. Product page

    What is inside, who it is for, the offer and consent to data processing.

  3. Contact for the receipt

    The buyer enters an email, we pass the order to YooKassa.

  4. Payment

    Payment by bank card on the secure YooKassa page.

  5. Access

    The «Thank you» page with a link to the product is shown only after a successful payment.

  6. Receipt and notification

    The buyer receives a tax receipt, the owner a notification.

  7. Refund

    A payment refund is made from the YooKassa dashboard.

Works, tested with test payments. The service runs in Yandex Cloud.
