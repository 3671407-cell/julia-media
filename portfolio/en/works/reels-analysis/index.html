What the numbers say

  • The best thousand: median 382 thousand views, minimum 106 thousand, maximum 38.6 million.
  • Date window: 4 August to 3 November 2026, videos in Russian (about 30%) and English (about 70%).
  • What leaders have in common: a short first line (about 50 characters), specifics (number, place, price), a cut-off phrase or a contrast, a video under 25 seconds.

What next

For each format we make 10 videos, count the median of views and scale the formats that pass the 10,000-view bar.
