Autoposting to all social networks

How posts go out by themselves and how you know everything is fine

  1. Schedule and queue

    Post texts wait in a queue with a date and time. Edits are made before publishing, nothing needs to be launched by hand.

  2. Hourly auto-launch

    In the cloud (GitHub Actions) checks every hour whether it is time to publish a post and starts the posting.

  3. Publishing

    Threads* through the official API, Telegram through the channel admin bot, VKontakte through the group key. Images and carousels come from a shared storage.

  4. Stop with one file

    A pause file stops all publications at once, for example while a text is being approved.

  5. Morning report

    At 9:00 a Telegram message arrives: views, followers and the best posts of the day.

  6. System check

    Keys and access are checked, and you learn in advance if a token is about to expire or a platform stopped responding.

Works on our own accounts. For a client we connect the same steps to their social networks.
