2.4. Answers to questions in the bot or in the form on the site (field of activity, task, budget).

3. Purposes of processing

3.1. Providing free materials (gifts) and access to the bot's functions.

3.2. Concluding and performing a contract: selling digital products, providing services, sending access to purchased materials.

3.3. Feedback: answers to questions and requests, consultations.

3.4. Sending informational and advertising messages, only with the user's separate consent to the mailing.

3.5. Improving the Services and analytics (in anonymized form).

4. Legal grounds

4.1. The user's consent to the processing of personal data.

4.2. A contract with the user (public offer) to which the user is a party.

5.1. Actions with data: collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, transfer (provision, access) in the cases from section 6, anonymization, blocking, deletion, destruction.

5.4. The Operator takes the necessary legal, organizational and technical measures to protect data from unlawful access, alteration, distribution and destruction.

6.3. The Operator does not sell or transfer personal data for the advertising of third parties.

7.1. Data is processed until the purposes of processing are achieved or until the user withdraws consent.

7.2. Purchase data is stored for the periods set by accounting and tax legislation.

7.3. After the purposes are achieved or consent is withdrawn, data is destroyed within 30 days, unless otherwise required by law.

The user has the right to:

To do this it is enough to write to director@law-buh.ru. The Operator replies within 10 working days. You can also unsubscribe from mailings with a command in the bot or the "Unsubscribe" button.

10.2. The current edition is always available via a link in the bot and on the site.
