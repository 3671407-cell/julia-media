Questions

Questions you wanted to ask

I have a small audience. Will this suit me?

Yes. The system exists precisely so that every person who arrives isn't lost. It's better to start before the audience gets big: the funnel and the bot will be ready for growth.

I don't understand tech

No technical brief is needed. At the review we ask questions and write the brief ourselves. From you: tell us about the product, answer questions and give access.

And if people don't buy?

We give no income guarantees, and nobody honestly can. We build the system, show the numbers at every step (how many came, how many reached a request) and strengthen what works.

Why is this better than a free bot builder?

A builder gives you a tool, and we give you a result: a product, funnel texts, a gift, warm-up, a configured client database and call booking. You don't spend weeks on setup and mistakes.

Why should we trust you if you've only just opened?

You can check for yourself: open our work, try the live bot and see how it is built. Price and scope are fixed before payment, you pay in stages. The contract is made with a sole proprietor.

How long will it take?

Site and requests: 5–7 days. Bot and funnel: 2–3 weeks. Turnkey system: 4–8 weeks.

What is a client database (CRM) in the bot?

It's your base of followers and clients inside the bot. You see who came and at which step, analyze the audience, write to people in direct messages and send mailings. A client can be booked for a call and the time goes into the calendar. All contacts stay with you. The set of features depends on the plan of the service the bot runs on; we'll pick a suitable one at the review.

What is an AI assistant in simple words?

It's a helper that understands questions in its own words and answers from your knowledge base: prices, services, rules. It honestly says it is an AI and passes complex questions to you.

What if Telegram is blocked?

Client contacts are stored with you, and the bot can be moved to another platform. We plan a backup channel in advance.

What if something breaks?

We fix things free for 2 weeks after launch. After that support starts from ₽10,000 a month, optional.
