Why do we put off a trip even though we really want it?

First reason: "it's not the time". Work, kids, chores. Time does not arrive by itself, it is assigned.

How to choose a hotel by the sea in winter so you do not regret it.

  1. Look at where the entrance to the water is. In winter the wind is stronger, and a sheltered bay saves the day.

  5. Choose breakfast on the terrace if there is one. A morning by the water sets the tone for the whole day.
