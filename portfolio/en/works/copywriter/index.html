Copywriter bot: a team of 13 agents

How a Telegram bot writes posts in the author's style and checks them

  1. Dialogue agent

    Talks in Telegram: buttons «New post», «Content plan», «Photo», «Trends».

  2. Orchestrator

    Takes the request, determines the task type and launches the right chain of agents.

  3. Architect and decomposer

    Plan the structure and split the task into small steps with a readiness criterion.

  4. Receiver and style analyst

    Read the author's channel and pick out her style: length, tone, repeating techniques.

  5. Copywriter and editor

    Write the post and make it more human, shorter and more precise.

  6. Anti-AI cleaner

    Removes words and turns of phrase that give away a machine text.

  7. Fact and rule checker

    Checks the text against confirmed facts and bans: prices, dates, promises.

  8. Tester and memory

    Accepts the text or sends it back for rework, and the author's replies are remembered for the next posts.

Works in a personal Telegram channel: the bot suggests a post, the author edits and approves it.
