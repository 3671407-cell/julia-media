The club's referral program: short and honest

Here is only what the company allows to be said publicly. I will share details and figures personally.

What it is

By becoming a club member, a person gets the opportunity to receive commission rewards for telling others about the platform and inviting them to use it. This is a referral subscription under the membership program.

Nine types of rewards

The program provides nine types of commission for expanding the number of platform users. We go through how they work in detail at a meeting.

Loyalty points

Points can be used to pay part of the company's products: Life Experience, hotels, cruises and other travel. And once a year a trip can be paid 100% with points.

This is not a job offer and not a promise of income. Results are different for everyone and depend on your actions. The program terms are set out in the offer at registration and in the member's personal account. I show the detailed marketing plan at a personal meeting.

Questions

You ask, I answer honestly

Is this a scam?
I understand that being cautious is normal. The company's information is public: registration, registers, insurance (see the "Don't take my word for it" block). Check it before talking to me. I promise nothing in advance.
Is it expensive?
The cost and points are explained in detail in the video "Club subscription". And on the call we will go through your trip.
I don't have time
You can book from your phone, and the call takes 20 minutes. The rest is at your own pace.
I don't know how to use such services
I will show you the platform step by step and help with your first bookings.
What if Booking is cheaper?
We compare identical conditions: the same hotel, dates and cancellation. The company has a best price guarantee. Found the same hotel cheaper elsewhere? Submit a request within 24 hours after booking. The company will refund 150% of the difference. Details are in the company's rules.
Can I get my money back?
These are three different things: membership (refund within 14 days under the rules, closing without a fee), booking (under the terms of the specific booking) and renewal (separate terms, we check with support).
What are points?
The company awards loyalty points for monthly payments. Points can be used to pay part of the company's products: Life Experience, hotels, cruises. Once a year a trip can be paid 100% with points. Points are not money and cannot be withdrawn to a card. Details in the video "Club subscription".
Is it suitable for a family?
Yes. A family of three or more can stay in villas, club hotels and in the "Resorts" section; accommodation is designed for 4–14 people. Children under 5 travel with their parents free of charge, under 12 pay 50%. Points can be spent on everyone if a villa or a Life Experience with large rooms is booked.
Who will help if I have a question?
I am available on Telegram, plus company support 24/7.
What if I change my mind after the call?
You say "no, thank you", and that's it.
